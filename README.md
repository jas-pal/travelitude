# Travelitude 🌍

Travelitude is a travel-planning platform that combines **trip planning, itinerary discovery, and travel-product recommendations** in one experience.

Instead of helping travelers plan an itinerary and then requiring them to search elsewhere for the products they need, Travelitude aims to connect the two:

> **Plan your trip. Discover what you need.**

## ✈️ Concept

Travelitude helps users:

- Explore travel destinations
- Build and discover travel itineraries
- Get recommendations based on their destination and activities
- Discover travel products relevant to their trip
- Eventually connect recommended products with affiliate marketplaces

For example, a user planning a winter trip to Finland could receive an itinerary along with recommendations for suitable winter clothing, hiking equipment, travel accessories, and other relevant products.

## 🚀 Current Prototype

The current version is a prototype demonstrating the core Travelitude experience.

### Current features

- Travel destination and itinerary experience
- Travel product recommendation concept

## 🧠 Future Vision

The longer-term goal is to make Travelitude more personalized and automated.

Potential future capabilities include:

- AI-assisted itinerary generation
- Destination-based product recommendations
- Activity-based recommendations
- Weather-aware recommendations
- Travel packing suggestions
- Travel insurance recommendations
- Integration with product marketplaces
- Affiliate product links
- Personalized travel profiles
- Automated trip planning
